Liftmap privacy policy

Effective 5 October 2026

Liftmap is a workout log that keeps your data on your phone.

What Liftmap stores

The workouts, sets, exercises, routines, bodyweight entries and settings you enter or import. They are stored only on your phone, apart from today's workout on your watch if you use Liftmap on one (below).

What Liftmap sends

Nothing, apart from today's workout to your own watch if you use Liftmap on one (below). Liftmap has no internet access (it doesn't request Android's internet permission), no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The developer never receives your data.

Your watch

If you use Liftmap on a Wear OS watch, today's workout goes between your phone and your watch, so the watch can show it and log your sets to the phone. It travels over Bluetooth or, when the two aren't connected by Bluetooth, through Google Play services' servers, end-to-end encrypted so that only your devices can read it. The watch keeps a copy of today's workout, and its tile and watch face show your next set. Liftmap never sends your data anywhere else.

Backups and exports you make

If you choose a backup folder or export a file, Liftmap writes it to the location you picked with Android's file picker, for example your phone's storage or a cloud folder you've connected. What happens to it there is up to you and that provider.

Android backup

If Android's own backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include Liftmap's data in your device backup to your Google account, end-to-end encrypted where your device supports it. You can turn this off in Android's settings.

Imports

When you import a file, Liftmap reads it on your phone. The file isn't sent anywhere.

Your lock screen and home screen

While you rest, the rest timer's notification shows the time left, including on the lock screen, and the home-screen widget shows your sessions this week and the muscles that need attention. Anyone who can see your screen can see them. You can turn Liftmap's notifications off in your phone's settings, and remove the widget like any other.

Permissions

Liftmap asks to send notifications (the rest timer, and on a watch its "Rest over" and the workout on the watch face) and uses vibration. If you allow it, it also uses exact alarms, so a rest ends on time while Liftmap is closed. It doesn't ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos, and it only reads the files you pick.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Liftmap deletes everything it stored on the phone, and uninstalling it from your watch deletes the watch's copy. You can also clear it in Android's app settings. Backup or export files you saved elsewhere stay where you put them.

Children

Liftmap isn't directed at children under 13.

Not medical advice

Liftmap is not a medical device and doesn't give medical advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here, and in the app under Settings › About Liftmap › Privacy policy.
